# Flickday identity exploration — July 2026

This folder preserves the first image-generation exploration against the current
Flickday Media visual language. These are concept rasters, not production logo
masters. The selected direction should be redrawn as controlled SVG paths before
replacing the live site, apparel, or overlay assets.

## Context used

- Live site: black, white, and Flickday Yellow `#facc15`; full-bleed action
  photography; Anton display type; Inter body type; JetBrains Mono labels.
- Current primary mark: lowercase `flickday` with a play triangle inside the `d`.
- Current alternate: lowercase `flickday` with a compact reel inside the `d`.
- Attached attempts: literal film-strip `d`, standalone reel-strip glyph, and a
  glossy 3D cinema treatment.
- Brand position: grassroots sports media — raw, fast, player-first; courtside,
  not corporate and not a traditional film studio.

## Files

- `01-play-d-first-round.png` — evolutionary play-`d` with a separate glyph and
  speed cut. Strong direction; the `f` slash is too generic.
- `02-fd-viewfinder-first-round.png` — bracketed capture monogram. Better as a
  secondary HUD/credential graphic than a primary logo.
- `03-reel-d-first-round.png` — simplified reel lineage. Useful as a heritage or
  production submark; still prioritizes cinema over grassroots sports.
- `04-editorial-cut-first-round.png` — condensed live-site response. Energetic,
  but too close to esports/gaming typography.
- `05-play-d-refined.png` — recommended concept direction. The `d`-play is the
  single idea; compact enough for the live header and simple enough for apparel.
- `references/` — user-provided attempts retained as visual-history inputs.

## Recommendation

Use a small identity family instead of making one mark carry every metaphor:

1. Primary wordmark: refined lowercase play-`d`.
2. Primary icon/avatar: extracted `d`-play glyph.
3. Heritage/production submark: simplified reel-`d`, used sparingly.
4. Supporting graphic language: viewfinder brackets, mono capture labels, hard
   yellow bars, and full-bleed action crops.

Do not use the glossy 3D treatment as the identity. It conflicts with the live
site, reproduces poorly, and reads as a traditional film-production company.

## Production gate

The generated concepts include subtle tonal variation even though flat output was
requested. That is acceptable for exploration only. A production master must be
redrawn into true flat SVG geometry, then tested at 16 px, 32 px, 121×40 px,
embroidery size, chest-print size, and as a semi-transparent video bug.
