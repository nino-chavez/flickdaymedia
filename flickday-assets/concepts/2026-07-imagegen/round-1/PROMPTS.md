# Image-generation prompts

Mode: built-in image generation using the user attempts and current Flickday
wordmarks as visual references.

## 01 — Play-d evolution

Exact lowercase `flickday`; heavy compact neo-grotesk; play triangle as negative
space in an unmistakable lowercase `d`; one restrained speed cut; flat Flickday
Yellow on black; designed for website header, apparel, avatar, and watermark.
Avoid film reels, film strips, lenses, gradients, 3D, extra copy, and mockups.

## 02 — FD capture monogram

Standalone `fd` capture/viewfinder monogram paired with exact lowercase
`flickday`; use negative space and a play wedge rather than a literal camera;
single-color yellow on black; legible at 32 px and stitchable. Avoid all film,
lens, aperture, ball, gradient, and 3D imagery.

## 03 — Simplified reel-d

Exact lowercase `flickday`; preserve reel-in-the-`d` lineage, but limit the bowl
to three large reel knockouts and the ascender to three oversized sprocket cuts;
no curling ribbon or external tail; one-color, small-size-safe, print-safe.

## 04 — Editorial capture cut

Exact lowercase `flickday`; condensed sports-magazine counterpart to the live
site typography; squared forms, hard terminals, tight spacing, one diagonal cut
and a small forward-facing wedge in the `d`; no film imagery or tech decoration.

## 05 — Refined play-d

Exact lowercase `flickday`; compact heavy lowercase neo-grotesk with restrained
forward stance; every letter disciplined except an unmistakable `d` containing a
single play triangle; no separate icon, divider, speed slash, film imagery, extra
copy, gradient, shadow, or 3D. This is the recommended direction to redraw.
