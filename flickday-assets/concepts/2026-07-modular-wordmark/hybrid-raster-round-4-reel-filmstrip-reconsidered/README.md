# Flickday reel and filmstrip reconsideration

Status: rejected direction studies, retained for decision history only.

## What changed

- `reel-d-counterform-v2.png` removes the pasted-in reel illustration and reduces the cue to three negative-space apertures inside the `d`.
- `filmstrip-y-frames-v2.png` preserves the normal `y` silhouette and cuts three frame windows into its descender.

## Assessment

The reel `d` is cleaner than round 3, but its three apertures can also read as a fan or rotor. The filmstrip `y` remains decorative rather than intrinsic to the letter. The prior reel-to-`y` combination compounds both problems and is retired rather than regenerated.

These studies support narrowing the working system to the core wordmark, play `d`, and aperture `i`. If a film-specific alternate is still needed, it should be developed as a separate emblem or motion signature rather than forced into the wordmark.

Do not use these raster proofs as production artwork.
