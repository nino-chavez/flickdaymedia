# Flickday identity reset — round 1

This round deliberately separates four decisions that had become tangled in earlier explorations:

1. the `flickday` wordmark;
2. the `MEDIA` descriptor;
3. the tagline `Every Day’s a Flickday`;
4. an optional independent mark.

## Current read

- **Advance 01 / Rush** as the lead wordmark territory. It has the strongest fit with the existing editorial-sports site and works as a compact video watermark.
- **Advance 02 / House** as the quieter control. It is less ownable, but more durable and legible.
- **Hold 03 / Day Block** as a campaign, apparel, and social-layout treatment rather than the master mark.
- **Hold 04 / Daily Index** as editorial furniture. The `24/7` chip is not part of the logo.
- **Do not approve an independent symbol yet.** Flick Trail is the most useful motion/device idea in this round, but it is not yet proprietary enough to become the permanent avatar or favicon.

## Copy check

Use:

- `Every Day’s a Flickday`
- `Raw. Fast. Player-first.`
- `Shot by Flickday`
- direct match-state language such as `Match Point`, `Set 03`, and event names

Avoid:

- `Everyday’s a Flickday` when the meaning is “each day”
- claims about “going viral”
- trend-chasing slang
- generic phrases such as `epic content` and `cinematic AF`

## Production note

These are review comps, not final logo masters. The selected wordmark needs optical refinement, outlined vector construction, one-color variants, small-size testing, and trademark screening before production use.
