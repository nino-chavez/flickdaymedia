# Flickday emblem exercise — round 2 audit

## Brief

Invent an independent Flickday emblem that fits the existing bold, modern gold/ink identity and can work on the website, apparel, social profiles, and video overlays. Avoid literal reel/filmstrip nostalgia and avoid cramming photo, video, and editing symbols into one mark.

## Concepts

### 01 — Flicked cap

**Advance.** This is the strongest connection to the brand because it uses the exact established `f`. Moving the cap makes the action more intentional than simply slicing the glyph. It can become a repeatable motion behavior: rest, flick, snap back.

Risk: a permanently detached cap can look broken. The gap and displacement need optical refinement, and the static mark must remain recognizable at 24–32 px.

### 02 — Frame echo

**Do not advance as a static emblem.** It is useful as a motion or transition treatment applied to the wordmark, but reads as a visual effect rather than a durable logo.

### 03 — Live frame

**Advance as an independent-symbol territory.** It communicates capture and motion without film nostalgia and works naturally as an app icon or social avatar.

Risk: open frames and escaping dots are common in technology branding. It needs a more proprietary proportion or relationship to the Flickday typography before adoption.

### 04 — Flick gesture

**Reject.** Dynamic, but too close to a sports-tech or performance-tracking symbol. It does not feel specifically connected to Flickday or media production.

### 05 — Moment field

**Reject.** Legible and functional, but essentially a camera focus interface. It is too generic to carry the identity.

### 06 — FD overlap

**Hold for refinement.** It has genuine name ownership and avoids media clichés. The current overlap is too dense, but a clearer single-color `fd` construction could become a restrained corporate/apparel monogram.

## Current ranking

1. Flicked cap — best brand ownership and best motion potential.
2. Live frame — clearest independent symbol, but needs differentiation.
3. FD overlap — strongest literal ownership, weakest current execution.

## Strategic conclusion

There are two valid paths, and they solve different problems:

- **Brand-led path:** advance the Flicked cap. Meaning comes from Flickday repeatedly using one recognizable letter action.
- **Category-led path:** advance Live frame. Meaning is more immediate, but distinctiveness must be designed into it.

The next round should refine those two rather than generating more unrelated icons. The FD monogram can remain a controlled third experiment for apparel and compact corporate placements.
