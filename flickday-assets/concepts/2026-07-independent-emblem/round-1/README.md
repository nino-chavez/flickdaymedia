# Flickday independent emblem exploration — round 1

Status: visual direction studies. Not production artwork.

## Strategic system

- Primary identity: neutral text-only `flickday` wordmark.
- Standalone emblem: separate from the letters and paired through a lockup.
- Approved specialist alternates remain the play-`d` video wordmark and aperture-`i` photography wordmark.
- Reel and filmstrip letter substitutions are retired.

## Concepts

### 01 — FD cut monogram

Two interlocked lowercase initials with one diagonal editing cut.

Assessment: hold/reject. The `d` can read as `b`, and the combined form is more complicated than it first appears. It is compact, but not immediately unambiguous.

### 02 — Frame Shift

Two offset rounded frames separated by a diagonal negative-space cut.

Assessment: reject as a primary identity. It is clean and scalable, but too generic; it resembles duplicate, layers, cards, or copy/paste UI iconography.

### 03 — Upright cut `f`

A standalone lowercase `f`, compatible with the wordmark, interrupted by one restrained diagonal frame-jump cut.

Assessment: advance. It is the simplest direction, retains clear Flickday ownership through the initial, survives reduction, supports motion, and avoids literal camera or cinema clip art. The cut is a brand gesture rather than an equipment illustration.

## Recommended next step

Reconstruct concept 03 as deterministic SVG geometry from the exact approved wordmark `f`. The final vector should use one flat gold, a slightly narrower cut, optically matched stem/crossbar weight, and true transparent negative space. Test standalone, horizontal, stacked, favicon, CapCut watermark, embroidery, and animated frame-jump applications.

Do not trace or ship the image-generated raster as the final logo.

## Generation method

Built-in image generation, one call per concept. Prompts held the palette to Flickday gold, prohibited literal camera/reel/filmstrip imagery, limited each mark to one idea, and required vector-friendly geometry. Flat chroma-key backgrounds were removed locally; the lockups were composed mechanically against one unchanged neutral wordmark.
