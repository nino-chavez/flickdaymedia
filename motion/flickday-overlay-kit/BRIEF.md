# Flickday HyperFrames pilot

## Objective

Build a small, reusable motion-graphics production layer for Flickday Media. The pilot proves three transparent 9:16 overlays intended for CapCut and other editors:

1. a clean animated Flickday wordmark sting;
2. a player lower third;
3. a compact highlight reaction stamp.

## Brand direction

- Grassroots sports media: raw, fast, player-first, courtside rather than corporate.
- Palette: black `#000000`, Flickday yellow `#FACC15`, white `#FFFFFF`, dark gray `#111111`.
- Use the established lowercase core wordmark unchanged.
- Use editorial timing, crop seams, sharp rules, and a selected-moment hit as the recurring graphic device.
- Do not use the rejected cut-`f`, a reel, a filmstrip, or a multi-icon wordmark.
- Avoid generic app-card styling, glossy 3D effects, gratuitous bounce, and faux-cinematic nostalgia.

## Outputs

- Canvas: 1080 × 1920, 30 fps.
- Export: transparent overlay, MOV/ProRes 4444 preferred for CapCut; PNG sequence is the fallback.
- All copy must remain concise, legible on phone video, contemporary, and free of forced slang.
- Demo copy may be used for proof frames, but every text field should be easy to replace in source.

## Composition briefs

### Wordmark sting

- Duration: 3.2 seconds.
- The clean wordmark resolves quickly and holds.
- A narrow yellow editorial seam supplies the signature motion; it must not become a new logo.
- Keep the center of the frame transparent so it can work as an intro, outro, or momentary watermark.

### Player lower third

- Duration: 4.5 seconds.
- Demo data: `MAYA SANTOS` and `12 · OUTSIDE`.
- Position in the lower third while preserving mobile safe areas and leaving the play action unobscured.
- Assertive typography, compact structure, no broadcast-news chrome.

### Highlight reaction stamp

- Duration: 2.2 seconds.
- Demo copy: `ACE`.
- Brief, emphatic arrival and clean disappearance; usable as a CapCut stamp over a point.
- No emoji, meme slang, exclamation pileup, flames, or victory-cliché copy.

## Run shape

- Workflow: motion-graphics.
- Mode: autonomous through checks and proof snapshots.
- Rendering remains approval-gated after verification.
