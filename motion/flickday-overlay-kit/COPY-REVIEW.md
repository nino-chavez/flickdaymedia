# Copy review

The overlays should sound like an editor labeling a moment, not a brand trying to imitate a comment section.

## Approved pilot copy

- `MAYA SANTOS` — demo player name; direct and factual.
- `12 · OUTSIDE` — compact role line; understandable to volleyball audiences without extra hype.
- `ACE` — immediate, accurate, and durable. The animation supplies the emphasis, so no exclamation mark is needed.

## Useful future stamp vocabulary

- `BLOCK`
- `KILL`
- `MATCH POINT`
- `GAME POINT`
- `ON THE LINE`
- `TOUCH`
- `TOOL`
- `CHASE`
- `RALLY`
- `POINT`

Use the sport-correct label only when the footage supports it. Short technical vocabulary feels current because it respects the audience.

## Avoid

- Forced slang: `SHEESH`, `ATE`, `NO CAP`, `IT'S GIVING`, `MAIN CHARACTER`.
- Empty superlatives: `INSANE`, `UNREAL`, `EPIC`, `LEGENDARY` unless the account voice already uses them sparingly.
- Aggressive or demeaning opponent copy: `DESTROYED`, `HUMILIATED`, `SENT HOME`, `VICTIM`.
- Emoji stacks, flame motifs, multiple exclamation marks, or hashtags inside the graphic.
- First-person claims that may be false when the template is reused.

## Rule of thumb

Name the play, the player, the score state, or the editorial context. Let the clip create the reaction.
